# Hack for Humanity: Miami

**Build what Miami needs next.** One day, four challenges, one city.

Hack for Humanity is [The AI Collective's global civic hackathon](https://fortune.com/press-releases/ai-collective-hack-for-humanity-global-civic-hackathon-2026-08-17/), running in 120 chapters across 50 countries this fall. Miami's edition is an official [MLH Hacktoberfest](https://hacktoberfest-handbook.mlh.com/) Hack Day: pick a challenge, build for nearly five hours, and demo a working answer to a real Miami problem. Every challenge comes from problems our community named during Humans in AI Week this summer.

- **When:** Saturday, October 10, 2026 · 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM ET
- **Where:** The LAB Miami, Wynwood (exact address on your Luma ticket)
- **Who:** Developers, designers, data people and anyone who knows a Miami problem up close
- **Format:** In person · solo or teams of up to 4 · all code written on the day
- **RSVP:** https://luma.com/h4h-miami

## The four challenges

Pick one track as your primary when you submit. Every project can also compete for the MLH Best Open-Source AI Project prize.

| # | Track | The question | You might build |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | [Four Forces, One City](Challenge%20Guide.md#track-01---four-forces-one-city) | How does Miami stay safe and livable as water, air, land and fire push back? | Street-level flood alerts, heat-safety copilots for job sites, storm-ready offline info hubs |
| 02 | [Wild Miami](Challenge%20Guide.md#track-02---wild-miami) | How do we protect the animals we share Miami with, and keep neighbors safe too? | Injured-wildlife reporting agents, roadkill hotspot maps, shelter adoption matchers |
| 03 | [Brain Gain](Challenge%20Guide.md#track-03---brain-gain) | How does Miami keep the talent it attracts? | Career pathway navigators, AI reskilling coaches, newcomer onboarding guides |
| 04 | [Data to Action](Challenge%20Guide.md#track-04---data-to-action) | What does the data say, and what should Miami do about it? | Dashboards, maps, alerts or agents built on open city data |

**City partner challenge:** a public-sector partner may add a challenge and datasets. Details drop before Oct 10, and using them is always optional.

## How it works

1. **Check in** through OrganizerHQ at the door. It's required to submit and to win.
2. **Form your team** of 1 to 4. Looking for teammates? Use the team-matching board in the lounge. Teams lock at 10:45 AM.
3. **Build** from 10:45 AM to 3:30 PM. Ideas and research from before the event are welcome; all code is written on the day.
4. **Submit** in OrganizerHQ by 3:45 PM: a public GitHub repo with an open-source license, a short description, the technologies you used and your track.
5. **Demo** for 2 minutes, plus 1 minute of questions. Show the working product, not slides.

Full rules and the submission checklist: [Build requirements](Challenge%20Guide.md#build-requirements).

## Judging

Judges score every demo from 1 to 5 on each criterion, and the weighted total decides the winners.

| Criterion | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Community impact | 30% |
| Working build | 25% |
| Use of AI and data | 20% |
| Originality and craft | 15% |
| Demo clarity | 10% |

What judges look for on each criterion: [Judging rubric](Challenge%20Guide.md#judging-rubric).

## Prizes

- **Grand Champion:** Arduino boards for the team
- **Track Champions (x4):** MLH Hacktoberfest swag and a spotlight on our community channels
- **MLH Best Open-Source AI Project:** a DEV Badge for every team member, plus MLH+DEV swag while supplies last
- **Every builder:** Hacktoberfest swag, plus an "I Demoed" sticker when your team presents

All winning teams may be invited to join a grant-funded project to commercialize what they built, and may be showcased in The AI Collective's global newsletter to 300k+ subscribers. Details: [Prize categories](Challenge%20Guide.md#prize-categories).
